# 网页版 ChatGPT：三组首页示例

[中文首页](../README.md) · [English overview](../README.en.md)

每组单独开启一个对话，上传指定照片后复制中文或英文提示词。中文、英文任选一种即可。

## 1. 十二款发型 / Twelve hairstyles

上传一张面部清楚、头部完整的正面或轻微侧面照片。Upload one clear portrait with the full head visible.

```text
请根据我上传的人物照片，生成一张4:5竖版发型目录，3列4行，共12格，从左上到右下编号1至12。
所有格子均为同一个人，保持面部特征、表情、肤色、服装、发色、妆容和配饰一致，背景统一为浅灰摄影棚。只改变头发的长度与造型。
12款发型依次为：精灵短发、齐下巴波波头、长波浪、低盘发、帘式刘海、马尾、法式波波头、长直发、狼尾、发髻、短卷发、复古大波浪。
每格使用相同机位与柔和灯光，头部完整，间距一致。只标编号，不添加其他文字。直接生成图片。
```

```text
Using my uploaded portrait as the identity reference, create a 4:5 portrait hairstyle lookbook with exactly 12 panels: 3 columns and 4 rows, numbered 1–12 from top left to bottom right.
Show the same person in every panel. Keep facial features, expression, skin tone, clothing, hair color, makeup and accessories consistent. Use the same light-gray studio background, camera angle and soft lighting. Change only hair length and hairstyle.
In order: pixie cut, chin-length bob, long waves, low bun, curtain bangs, ponytail, French bob, long straight hair, wolf cut, chignon, short curls and vintage Hollywood waves.
Keep the entire head visible with consistent framing and spacing. Add only the panel numbers. Generate the finished image directly.
```

## 2. 旅行珐琅徽章 / Travel enamel pin

上传一张有地标或风景的旅行照片，可以有人。Upload a travel photo showing a landscape or landmark; people are optional.

```text
请把我上传的旅行照片转成一枚旅行纪念珐琅徽章，生成1:1方形图片。
让照片中的地形、风景或地标构成徽章主体和异形外轮廓。如果照片有人物，将人物保留为场景中的较小角色，不要改成单人人像徽章。
用细金色金属线分隔珐琅色块，保留照片中的主要颜色、人物服装、发色和自然肤色。珐琅表面细腻光亮，有真实厚度和柔和反光。
徽章居中放在深蓝色粗织布上，占画面约60%，四周留白，底部有自然接触阴影。不要矩形相框，不添加文字。直接生成图片。
```

```text
Transform my uploaded travel photo into a souvenir enamel pin, presented in a square 1:1 image.
Use the defining landscape, terrain or landmark to form the main body and irregular outer silhouette of the pin. If people appear in the photo, retain them as smaller figures within the scene rather than turning the pin into an isolated portrait.
Separate enamel color areas with thin gold metal outlines. Preserve the main colors, clothing, hair colors and natural skin tones from the photo. Give the enamel subtle gloss, believable thickness and soft reflections.
Center the pin on dark navy coarse woven fabric, occupying about 60% of the image, with generous space around it and a natural contact shadow. No rectangular frame or added text. Generate the image directly.
```

## 3. 薄荷玫瑰香水 / Perfume with mint roses

上传一张瓶身和标签清楚的香水照片。Upload a clear product photo with the bottle and label visible.

```text
请以我上传的香水照片为商品参考，制作一张2:3竖版香水广告图。
保持原香水瓶的造型、比例、实际玻璃颜色、瓶盖材质、标签图案和文字。不要把瓶身改成薄荷绿色，也不要重新设计包装。
香水瓶放在画面中央，一条米色织带从瓶后自然绕过，不遮住正面标签。周围点缀薄荷绿色玫瑰和浅绿色植物，花朵与枝叶不遮挡瓶身主体。
背景为冷调浅色渐变，柔和逆光，玻璃有自然通透感和细腻反射，背景适度虚化，商品清楚锐利。只改变场景和布光，不增加广告文字。直接生成图片。
```

```text
Use my uploaded perfume photo as the product reference and create a 2:3 portrait perfume advertisement.
Preserve the bottle shape, proportions, actual glass color, cap material, label artwork and lettering. Do not recolor the bottle mint green or redesign its packaging.
Place the bottle at the center. Wrap a beige fabric ribbon naturally behind it without covering the front label. Arrange mint-green roses and pale-green foliage around the bottle, keeping the main product unobstructed.
Use a cool, pale gradient background, soft backlighting, natural glass transparency and refined reflections. Keep the product sharp with a gently blurred background. Change only the setting and lighting. Add no advertising text. Generate the image directly.
```
